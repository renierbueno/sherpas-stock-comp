# 12 · Stock compensation rules

The US tax and legal rules the model applies, one row each, with the research entry it stands on and how strong that source is.
Every decision, flow-review gap, spec line and simulator constant that depends on a US tax or legal rule cites one `SC-NN`; `npm run check` checks the citations, and `tests/unit/rules.test.ts` checks that the Value column equals the simulator's constants (D-100).

How to read it: Signal is the weaker of the rule's sources (`research.md`). A Release 1 rule with a WEAK signal cites the assumption it rests on. Release 1 is the first release of D-080 (RSU and PSU of listed employers, tax at vest, withholding and gap, the FICA line, the Liquidity check, the employer concentration flag); Next is everything after it (projection, 409A, cost basis, shares kept, options, ESPP, RSA, SAR, ESOP). Tax year "All" means the rule is in the statute or regulation and not indexed. Not tax advice.
