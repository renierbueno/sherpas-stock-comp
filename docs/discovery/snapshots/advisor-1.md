## Stories

### The grant that showed up in the second meeting
- **Context:** a new prospect, two earners, one at a public tech company. Intake form filled by the client.
- **What happened:** the form had one income box; the client entered base salary only. In the second meeting the client opened the stock-plan portal on their phone: four RSU grants, quarterly vesting, plus ESPP lots. Dana copied the grants into a spreadsheet by hand.
- **Workaround / pain:** the analysis already sent to the client showed a diversified portfolio; the unvested grants and the employer link were missing. Dana had to explain why the second version looked riskier.

### Tax surprise in April
- **Context:** an existing client whose RSUs vested through the year with shares withheld for tax at 22%.
- **What happened:** the client's marginal rate was higher, so withholding fell short (R-008 shows the 22% default); the client owed tax in April and called Dana upset.
- **Workaround / pain:** Dana now asks for the vest calendar every January, but only for clients she remembers have equity.
