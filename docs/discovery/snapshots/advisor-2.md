## Stories

### Double counting after linking accounts
- **Context:** a new client links her brokerage account during onboarding.
- **What happened:** the shares already vested appear twice: once as a brokerage position and once in the RSU entry he had typed in. Net worth jumps by the value of those shares.
- **Workaround / pain:** he deletes the vested part of the RSU entry by hand and keeps only unvested tranches there; future vests go in as "other income" so the cash-flow projection moves.

### Sell at vest or hold
- **Context:** an annual review, the client's company stock is up a lot.
- **What happened:** he builds two scenarios by hand, sell everything at vest vs hold two more years, to show the tax and the concentration side by side.
- **Workaround / pain:** "takes some work to set up" every year, and the client asks for it again at each vest.
