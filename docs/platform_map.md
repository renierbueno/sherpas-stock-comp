What the Sherpas demo platform shows, screen by screen, as seen on 2026-10-03. Our own notes; raw captures stay in `captures/` (outside git). One line per screen: view · screen · what it shows · what the user can do · open question it answers.

## Scope of what was captured

The demo login is the **end client's** view of a shared analysis, not the advisor's workspace. The user is the household head with a client role; "Switch workspace" offers only "Add an account". The advisor side (opportunity pipeline, Data & Assumptions, manual score adjustment, questionnaire builder) is not reachable from this login. [FACT: captures/client/05-avatar-menu] [FACT: captures/client/01-financial-health]

The demo household: a couple aged 40 and 38 with three children, $315K salaries plus $24K rental income, $2.05M assets, $188K liabilities, five goals (retire 2046 at 60/58, three college funds, a home in 2030, a vehicle in 2028, a yearly vacation). [FACT: captures/client/01-financial-health, 04-statements-balance]
